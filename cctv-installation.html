SYSTEM DESIGN

CCTV Site Assessment and Camera Coverage Planning

A useful CCTV design begins with the areas to be observed, the purpose of surveillance, site layout, lighting, mounting positions, privacy requirements and the level of detail needed in recorded images.

Planning may include reviewing floor plans, identifying entrances and exits, reception areas, corridors, perimeter zones, loading bays and other agreed locations. Final camera positions, quantities and fields of view should be confirmed through a site assessment rather than estimated from building size alone.

CAMERA DEPLOYMENT

IP CCTV and HD Camera Installation

We support deployment of IP camera systems and compatible HD analogue surveillance solutions, selected to suit the project specification and existing infrastructure.

Camera options may include dome, turret, bullet or PTZ models where appropriate. Selection considers indoor or outdoor use, lighting, weather exposure, viewing angle, image detail, mounting and the intended monitoring area.

RECORDING & STORAGE

NVR, DVR and CCTV Recording Configuration

Recording equipment and storage should be selected around the required camera channels, image settings, recording schedule and footage retention target.

Project scope may include NVR or DVR installation, drive configuration, recording schedules, playback checks, user permissions and basic system documentation. Retention capacity must be calculated using the actual camera and recording parameters.

NETWORK CONNECTIVITY

CCTV Cabling, PoE and Network Integration

IP surveillance systems depend on a suitable network and power design. Works may include Ethernet cabling, PoE switching where supported, fibre uplinks between buildings, equipment rack organisation and network configuration.

Where required, CCTV traffic can be separated from other network services using appropriate VLANs, addressing and access policies. See our structured cabling, fibre optic and network infrastructure services.

REMOTE ACCESS

Remote Viewing and Secure User Access

Remote viewing can be configured where the selected recorder or camera platform supports it and the site has suitable internet and network connectivity.

Setup may include authorised user accounts, mobile or desktop client configuration, permissions and secure access practices. Remote access availability depends on the equipment, internet service, network policy and manufacturer platform; it is not a substitute for a properly scoped monitoring service.

LIFECYCLE SUPPORT

CCTV Maintenance, Troubleshooting and Upgrades

Existing systems may require fault diagnosis, camera replacement, recording or playback checks, storage upgrades, network corrections, configuration review or expansion to additional coverage areas.

Maintenance scope can include inspection of cameras, mounts, connectors, cabling, power supplies, recorder health, date and time settings, recording schedules and remote access. Recommendations follow assessment of the installed system.

CCTV Solutions for Different Property Types

Commercial buildings and offices

Surveillance planning for entrances, reception areas, workspaces, corridors, parking and other approved common areas, with recording and access controls suited to business operations.

Schools and education institutions

Camera coverage for approved access points, shared facilities, corridors, perimeters and other designated areas, with careful consideration of privacy and safeguarding requirements.

Warehouses and industrial facilities

Surveillance for loading zones, stock areas, vehicle access, boundaries and operational spaces, taking account of lighting, dust, weather exposure and mounting conditions.

Residential properties and estates

CCTV for homes, apartment blocks and managed residential developments, with coverage designed around entrances, parking, shared spaces and property boundaries.

Our CCTV Installation and Upgrade Process

1. Requirements and site review

Discuss security objectives, review the site layout, identify coverage areas and assess lighting, mounting, cabling, power and network conditions.

2. System proposal and deployment

Agree camera types, recorder capacity, storage target, cabling, network arrangements, installation scope, equipment and project schedule.

3. Configuration, testing and handover

Configure cameras and recording, verify live view and playback, test agreed remote access, set up authorised users and hand over operating information.

What to Include in a CCTV Installation Enquiry

Providing these details helps us assess the requirement and prepare a practical proposal:

  • Project location, property type and number of buildings or floors.
  • Floor plans, site layout or photographs showing the areas to be monitored.
  • Priority coverage areas, indoor/outdoor requirements and any known blind spots.
  • Approximate camera quantity, if known, and whether this is a new installation or upgrade.
  • Preferred camera types or existing equipment make and model, if available.
  • Required recording retention period and whether continuous or motion-based recording is preferred.
  • Existing cabling, network switches, PoE, fibre backbone, power and internet availability.
  • Project timeline, BOQ/tender specification and maintenance requirements.

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of CCTV systems can Mlina install?

The solution may include IP or HD analogue cameras, depending on the existing infrastructure, image requirements, recording platform, budget and project specification. Camera selection and quantities are confirmed after reviewing the site and coverage objectives.

Can you install CCTV in schools, offices and warehouses?

Yes. CCTV systems can be planned for schools, offices, warehouses, commercial buildings, residential properties and institutions. Camera positions, coverage, privacy considerations, cabling and recording requirements depend on the site.

Do you install NVR and DVR recording systems?

Yes. Where included in the project scope, we install or configure compatible NVRs for IP camera systems and DVRs for supported analogue or HD analogue systems.

Can I view CCTV cameras remotely on my phone?

Remote viewing can be configured where the cameras or recorder, network, internet connection and selected manufacturer platform support it. Secure account configuration and appropriate network controls should be included in the handover.

How long will CCTV footage be retained?

Retention depends on storage capacity, camera count, resolution, frame rate, compression, motion settings and recording schedule. Specify the target retention period so storage can be sized accordingly.

Do you provide CCTV maintenance and upgrades?

Maintenance and upgrade work may include camera and recorder checks, cleaning, connector and cabling inspection, storage and playback checks, configuration review, fault diagnosis and component replacement, subject to assessment.

Where does Mlina provide CCTV installation services?

Mlina Networking Technologies Ltd is based in Ongata Rongai, Nairobi County, and undertakes ICT and ELV projects across Kenya, subject to project scope and logistics.

What information is needed for a CCTV quotation?

Share the site location, building type, floor plans or site layout, areas to be monitored, approximate camera count if known, indoor or outdoor requirements, desired recording retention, existing network and power infrastructure, and project timeline.
