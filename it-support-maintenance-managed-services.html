USER & DEVICE SUPPORT

Business IT Helpdesk and Technical Support

Assist users with day-to-day ICT issues affecting computers, connectivity, printers, business applications and supported peripherals.

Support arrangements can include issue logging, initial diagnosis, remote guidance, escalation, on-site visits where needed and closure notes. Supported devices, users, service channels and operating hours are defined in the service agreement.

REMOTE ASSISTANCE

Remote IT Support and Troubleshooting

Resolve suitable technical issues remotely where secure connectivity, user consent and authorised access are available.

Remote assistance may cover connectivity checks, supported device configuration, application troubleshooting, network diagnostics and guided user support. Access should use approved tools, least-privilege permissions and agreed session controls.

ON-SITE SERVICES

On-Site IT and Network Support

Arrange technical visits for faults or tasks that require physical access to equipment, cabling, network rooms or user workstations.

On-site work may include connectivity troubleshooting, device replacement coordination, patching checks, rack inspection, equipment installation support and verification. Scheduling depends on location, access, issue priority and agreed terms.

PREVENTIVE CARE

Scheduled ICT Preventive Maintenance

Reduce avoidable disruption through planned checks of supported ICT equipment and infrastructure.

Maintenance activities may include device health checks, physical inspection, ventilation and housekeeping checks, firmware and patch planning, configuration review, capacity observations and maintenance reports. Tasks are selected according to manufacturer guidance and the operating environment.

NETWORK OPERATIONS

LAN, WAN and Wi-Fi Maintenance

Maintain business connectivity across routers, switches, wireless access points, structured cabling and fibre links.

Support may include link and interface checks, addressing and DHCP/DNS troubleshooting, VLAN and routing review, wireless connectivity diagnosis, configuration backup and fault escalation to internet or carrier providers where required.

ELV SYSTEM SUPPORT

CCTV, Access Control and Communications Support

Coordinate technical support for supported electronic security and communication systems, including CCTV, access control, intruder alarms, IP PBX and VoIP.

Work may include fault diagnosis, connectivity checks, configuration review, device status checks and maintenance coordination. Platform access, software licences, manufacturer support and third-party subscriptions remain subject to the agreed scope.

BACKUP & DOCUMENTATION

Configuration Backups and IT Documentation

Maintain agreed technical records to support continuity, troubleshooting and controlled changes.

Documentation may include device inventories, network diagrams, IP and VLAN schedules, configuration backup records, maintenance logs, known-issue registers and change notes. Backup storage, retention, encryption and restore testing must be explicitly defined.

SERVICE MANAGEMENT

Managed IT and SLA-Based Support

For organisations seeking ongoing assistance, support can be structured as a recurring maintenance or managed-service arrangement.

An SLA can define covered equipment and locations, support hours, contact channels, response and escalation targets, planned visits, reporting, exclusions and fees. Service levels apply only when agreed in writing and depend on access, dependencies and the selected coverage.

How Our IT Support Arrangements Work

1. Discover and assess

Review your users, sites, devices, network environment, critical services, recurring issues, support history and existing service providers.

2. Define coverage and service levels

Agree supported assets, locations, support hours, contact methods, remote access arrangements, planned maintenance, escalation paths, reporting and exclusions.

3. Support, maintain and report

Deliver the agreed support activities, record incidents and maintenance work, communicate outstanding risks and review service requirements periodically.

What to Include in an IT Support Enquiry

Share the following information where available to help us scope a practical support arrangement:

  • Organisation type, site locations, number of users and operating hours.
  • Approximate number and types of computers, servers, printers and network devices.
  • Router, switch, Wi-Fi, firewall, CCTV, access control, IP PBX and UPS equipment details where support is required.
  • Current network diagrams, asset lists, service-provider details and existing support contracts, if available.
  • Recurring faults, known performance issues, outage history and business-critical applications.
  • Required remote and on-site support coverage, preferred contact channels and escalation contacts.
  • Preventive maintenance frequency, reporting expectations and documentation requirements.
  • Desired support hours, priority definitions and any proposed response targets.
  • Backup requirements, retention expectations and responsibility for restore testing.

Security note: Do not submit passwords, private keys, recovery codes, configuration secrets or sensitive personal data through a public enquiry form. Access credentials should be exchanged only through an approved secure process.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does business IT support include?

Depending on the agreement, support may include helpdesk coordination, remote troubleshooting, on-site technical visits, user and device support, network fault diagnosis, maintenance, configuration backups and service reporting.

Do you provide remote and on-site IT support?

Remote support may be provided where secure access, user authorisation and suitable connectivity are available. On-site support can be arranged subject to location, issue, availability and agreed terms.

Can you provide scheduled preventive maintenance?

Maintenance plans can be scoped around supported equipment and may include health checks, cleaning where appropriate, firmware planning, configuration backups, capacity checks and reports.

Do you offer service-level agreements?

Support can be structured under an agreed SLA defining covered systems, support hours, response and escalation targets, service channels, exclusions, reporting and charges. No response time or uptime commitment applies unless documented in the agreement.

Do you monitor systems 24 hours a day?

Continuous 24-hour monitoring is not automatically included. Monitoring tools, alert handling, operating hours, escalation and out-of-hours response must be separately assessed and agreed.

Can you support networks, CCTV, access control and IP PBX?

Support can be scoped for supported ICT and ELV infrastructure such as LAN/WAN, Wi-Fi, fibre links, CCTV, access control, intruder alarms, IP PBX and UPS systems, subject to access, compatibility and agreed coverage.

Do you manage backups and cybersecurity?

Configuration backup and coordination of agreed backup procedures may form part of a support plan. Backup storage, retention, restore testing, endpoint security licences, incident response and security monitoring must be defined separately.
