Website Design, Hosting & Business IT Services in Kenya

Give your organisation a professional online presence and dependable digital foundations. Mlina Networking Technologies helps businesses plan and develop websites, coordinate domains and hosting, configure professional email and maintain the IT services that support day-to-day operations.

BUSINESS WEBSITE DESIGN

Professional Website Design and Development

We help organisations present their services, capabilities and contact information through clear, responsive business websites. Project planning considers your audience, business objectives, content, page structure and the actions you want visitors to take.

Depending on the brief, a website may include company profiles, service pages, product or solution listings, project portfolios, enquiry pathways, news or resource sections and links to relevant business platforms. Design, functionality and delivery scope are agreed before development.

DOMAINS & DEPLOYMENT

Domain Registration, DNS and Website Launch

Your domain is an important part of your organisation's digital identity. We can assist with domain registration support, DNS configuration, domain connections and deployment of a completed website to its agreed hosting environment.

We also help review renewal responsibilities, access ownership, SSL configuration and launch requirements so that the handover is clear. Domain registration and third-party subscription costs are confirmed separately where applicable.

WEB HOSTING

Web Hosting and Server Support

We help assess hosting requirements for business websites, considering expected traffic, storage, application needs, email arrangements, backup requirements and the level of administration required.

Hosting support may include account and DNS coordination, website migration planning, deployment assistance, SSL setup and troubleshooting. The hosting provider, package, resource limits, service availability and renewal terms should be documented in the proposal.

BUSINESS COMMUNICATION

Professional Business Email Setup

Use email addresses associated with your organisation's domain to support a consistent business identity. We can assist with mailbox planning, domain email configuration, DNS records and setup on supported computers and mobile devices.

Platform selection, mailbox quantities, storage, security features, licensing and recurring subscription costs depend on the organisation's requirements and the selected email provider.

WEBSITE CARE

Website Maintenance, Updates and Security

A website needs ongoing attention after launch. Depending on the agreed maintenance plan, support can include content changes, software or plugin updates where applicable, backup checks, troubleshooting, basic security reviews and performance improvements.

We recommend defining update frequency, backup arrangements, recovery responsibilities, access controls and support response expectations before maintenance begins. Emergency recovery and major redesign work may require a separate scope.

ICT CONSULTANCY & SUPPORT

Business IT Support and Technology Advice

We help businesses assess practical technology requirements and resolve agreed ICT issues affecting their digital operations. This may include workstation and connectivity troubleshooting, business email support, website-related assistance and coordination with wider network infrastructure.

For organisations planning new systems or upgrades, we can help review requirements, identify dependencies and develop a practical implementation scope. Support availability and service levels are agreed according to the engagement.

Our Website and Business IT Project Process

1. Discovery and requirements

Discuss your organisation, audience, goals, existing website or domain, required pages, features, email needs, hosting expectations and timeline.

2. Proposal and implementation

Agree the deliverables, platform, content responsibilities, third-party subscriptions and project milestones, then carry out the approved design, configuration or support work.

3. Review, launch and handover

Review the completed work, check agreed functionality and mobile presentation, coordinate launch and provide the relevant access, documentation and ongoing support arrangements.

What to Include in a Website or Business IT Enquiry

Sharing a few details helps us understand the work and prepare a more relevant proposal:

  • Your organisation name, industry and primary website or IT objectives.
  • Whether this is a new website, redesign, migration or support request.
  • Required pages, features, integrations and examples of websites you like.
  • Your preferred domain name, if available, and any existing domain or hosting arrangements.
  • Business email requirements, including approximate mailbox quantities.
  • Whether you have approved text, photographs, logos and other website content ready.
  • Your preferred timeline, ongoing maintenance expectations and budget range, if established.
Account security:

Please do not include passwords, payment card details or private account credentials in an initial enquiry. Access arrangements can be agreed securely if a project proceeds.

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of business websites can Mlina help create?

Depending on your brief, we can help plan and develop company profile websites, service and product websites, portfolio sites, landing pages and other business-focused web experiences. Features and integrations are agreed during project scoping.

Do you assist with domain registration and web hosting?

Yes. We provide domain and hosting support, including assessment of suitable arrangements, DNS requirements, renewals and website deployment. Ownership, provider, subscription terms and resources should be confirmed in the proposal.

Can you set up professional business email?

We can assist with domain-based mailboxes, DNS records and configuration on supported devices or email applications. The email platform, mailbox capacity and licensing depend on your requirements.

Do you maintain and update existing websites?

Website care can include agreed content updates, software and plugin updates where applicable, backups, basic security checks, troubleshooting and performance reviews. The maintenance scope is defined before work begins.

Can you improve or redesign an existing website?

Yes. We can review design, mobile usability, content structure, navigation and technical issues, then recommend improvements or a redesign based on your current platform and business objectives.

Do you provide IT support for small and medium-sized businesses?

We can discuss practical IT support and consultancy, including workstation and network connectivity issues, business email, website-related support and ICT infrastructure coordination. Arrangements depend on scope and agreed service levels.

Where does Mlina provide website and business IT services?

We are based in Ongata Rongai, Nairobi County, and undertake ICT projects and support engagements across Kenya, subject to project scope and logistics.

What information should I provide when requesting a quotation?

Share your website goals, required pages and features, domain or hosting details where relevant, reference websites, content availability and timeline. Please do not send passwords in an initial enquiry.
