ABOUT MLINA NETWORKING TECHNOLOGIES

Connecting Technology to the Way You Work.

We deliver practical ICT infrastructure, electronic security, networking and communication solutions that help organisations connect, operate securely and grow with confidence.

Who We Are

Mlina Networking Technologies Ltd. is an ICT and ELV infrastructure solutions company focused on helping businesses, educational institutions, commercial facilities and organisations establish dependable technology environments.

Our areas of expertise span structured copper and fibre optic cabling, enterprise networking, campus Wi-Fi, CCTV surveillance, access control, intruder alarm systems, IP PBX, UPS and power protection, website hosting and IT infrastructure support.

We recognise that every organisation has different operational requirements. Our approach is therefore centred on understanding the client's environment, identifying appropriate technologies, defining a practical implementation scope and delivering solutions with attention to quality, reliability and long-term maintainability.

Our Mission

To provide dependable, practical and professionally delivered ICT and electronic security infrastructure solutions that empower organisations to operate efficiently, communicate effectively and protect their critical assets.

Our Vision

To become a trusted technology infrastructure partner for organisations seeking reliable connectivity, integrated security, modern communication systems and sustainable ICT solutions.

Our Technical Capabilities

Our service portfolio covers the key technology infrastructure required by modern organisations.

  • Structured copper cabling, fibre optics and FTTH infrastructure.
  • OTDR testing, fibre fault identification, termination and documentation.
  • Enterprise LAN, WAN, routing, switching and network configuration.
  • Campus Wi-Fi, wireless bridges and hotspot deployment.
  • IP CCTV surveillance, recording and remote monitoring.
  • Biometric access control and electronic door security.
  • Intruder alarm and security detection systems.
  • Alcatel, Grandstream and Yeastar IP PBX and VoIP systems.
  • UPS installation, power backup and ICT equipment protection.
  • Website design, hosting, business email and IT support.
