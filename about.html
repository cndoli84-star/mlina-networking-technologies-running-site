Who we are

Mlina Networking Technologies Ltd. is an ELV and ICT infrastructure brand focused on helping organisations build dependable connectivity, safer premises and effective communication environments. Our service scope includes CCTV and surveillance, campus Wi-Fi, structured cabling and fibre, access control, intruder alarms, IP PBX, UPS and power, hosting and IT solutions.

Our approach places the client's operational requirements first: understand the environment, define the right scope, deploy carefully and support the solution after handover.
