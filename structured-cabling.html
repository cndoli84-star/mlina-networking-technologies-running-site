COPPER CABLING

Cat6, Cat6A and Cat7 Network Cabling

We support structured cabling deployments using the cable category and components specified for the application. Cat6, Cat6A and Cat7 requirements should be assessed as a complete cabling system, including cable, connectors, outlets, patch panels and installation practices.

Design considerations include application requirements, channel length, pathway conditions, expected network speeds, electromagnetic environment and future expansion. The selected category should be consistent across compatible components and the project specification.

POINTS & TERMINATION

Data Outlets, Faceplates and Patch Panels

Structured cabling provides fixed connection points for computers, printers, IP phones, wireless access points, CCTV cameras and other compatible network devices.

Scope can include information outlets, faceplates, keystone jacks, patch panels, rack-side termination, outlet identification and consistent labelling at both ends of each link.

RACKS & CABINETS

Network Racks and Equipment Organisation

We support the installation and organisation of network cabinets and racks to bring patching and network equipment together in an accessible location.

Depending on the design, works may include rack installation, patch panels, horizontal and vertical cable managers, equipment shelves, PDU coordination, earthing provisions and orderly patch-cord routing.

PATHWAYS & CONTAINMENT

Cable Trays, Trunking and Conduits

Appropriate pathways help protect cabling, maintain separation from other building services where required and support future maintenance.

Installation planning may cover trunking, conduits, cable trays, basket trays, sleeves, risers and coordination with ceilings, floors and existing building infrastructure. Final routing is subject to site conditions and approved drawings.

QUALITY CHECKS

Cable Testing and Link Verification

Testing verifies installed links against the requirements selected for the cabling system. The test method, parameters, limits and reporting format should be agreed before installation and acceptance.

Depending on the scope and equipment, testing may include wire-map checks, length and performance tests, troubleshooting and recorded results. Formal certification should only be stated where the relevant system, test method and certification requirements have actually been met.

DOCUMENTATION

Labelling, Records and Handover

Clear identification makes it easier to trace outlets, patch-panel ports and network connections during moves, changes and fault resolution.

Handover deliverables may include outlet schedules, rack elevations, cable identifiers, test results, marked-up drawings and outstanding-works records, according to the agreed project requirements.

Our Structured Cabling Project Process

1. Requirements and site assessment

Review floor plans, outlet quantities, equipment rooms, pathways, cable categories, building conditions and project specifications.

2. Installation and termination

Coordinate approved routes, cable installation, containment, outlet and rack termination, dressing and labelling.

3. Testing and handover

Perform the agreed link tests, resolve identified issues within scope and prepare the specified test records and handover documents.

What to Include in a Structured Cabling Enquiry

For a more accurate assessment and quotation, share the details available to you:

  • Project location, building type and number of floors or blocks.
  • Floor plans, reflected ceiling plans or marked-up network layouts, if available.
  • Estimated number of data points and intended device types.
  • Required cable category and any specified manufacturer or system.
  • Rack locations, rack sizes and available power or equipment-room details.
  • Existing pathways, containment requirements and any civil or builder's works interfaces.
  • Testing, documentation, project schedule and BOQ requirements.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in structured cabling installation?

Depending on the project scope, this may include pathway coordination, cable installation, outlets, patch panels, racks, termination, labelling, testing and handover records.

Do you install Cat6, Cat6A and Cat7 cabling?

We can work with Cat6, Cat6A and Cat7 requirements where specified. The appropriate category and components should be selected as a complete link or channel design based on application, distance, performance and project specification.

Do you provide cable testing and certification?

Testing can be included in the agreed scope using suitable test equipment and limits for the selected cabling system. Deliverables and any formal third-party certification requirements should be agreed before work begins.

Can structured cabling support CCTV, Wi-Fi and IP phones?

A properly planned cabling system can provide network connections for compatible CCTV cameras, wireless access points, IP phones, computers and other network devices, subject to design and power requirements.

Where does Mlina provide structured cabling services?

We are based in Ongata Rongai, Nairobi County, and undertake ICT and ELV projects across Kenya, subject to project scope and logistics.

What details are needed for a quotation?

Share the site location, building or floor plans if available, approximate data-point count, cable category, rack locations, pathway conditions, testing requirements, BOQ and project timeline.
