WIRELESS PLANNING

Wi-Fi Coverage Planning and Site Assessment

A dependable wireless network starts with understanding the building, construction materials, user density, applications, existing network and coverage expectations.

Planning may include floor-plan review, access point positioning, indoor and outdoor coverage requirements, interference considerations, capacity planning and a site survey where appropriate. Final access point quantities and locations should be confirmed against site conditions and the agreed performance objectives.

ACCESS POINTS

Enterprise Wireless Access Point Installation

We support the installation and configuration of wireless access points for business and institutional environments, with centralised management options where supported by the selected platform.

Works may include mounting, cabling coordination, PoE arrangements, device adoption, firmware planning, SSID configuration, channel and transmit-power settings, and basic operational checks.

CAMPUS CONNECTIVITY

Multi-Building and Campus Wi-Fi

Schools, colleges, hotels and large facilities may require wireless coverage across multiple buildings, floors, common areas and outdoor spaces.

We can help plan access point distribution and the network backhaul needed to connect coverage zones. Depending on distance and site layout, the backbone may use structured copper cabling, fibre optic links or suitable wireless bridges.

GUEST ACCESS

Guest Wi-Fi, Hotspots and User Access

Guest wireless services can be separated from internal business networks using suitable SSIDs, VLANs, firewall policies and authentication methods.

Where supported by the selected platform, a deployment may include captive portal or hotspot access, voucher or account-based authentication, bandwidth limits, session policies and usage controls. Payment integration is subject to a separately agreed scope and supported payment provider.

WIRELESS BACKHAUL

Point-to-Point and Point-to-Multipoint Links

Wireless bridges can provide connectivity between buildings or remote network points where installing physical cabling is difficult or uneconomical.

Feasibility depends on line of sight, Fresnel clearance, distance, frequency conditions, interference, capacity, mounting and regulatory requirements. A site assessment is recommended before selecting equipment or promising throughput.

NETWORK OPTIMISATION

Wireless Security, Roaming and Performance

We support practical configuration of wireless security, network segmentation and performance settings to suit the environment and selected equipment.

Scope may include WPA2/WPA3 settings where supported, guest and staff network separation, VLAN mapping, access policies, roaming configuration, channel planning, interference checks and troubleshooting.

Our Enterprise Wi-Fi Deployment Process

1. Requirements and site review

Establish coverage zones, user density, device types, applications, existing internet service, network infrastructure and security requirements.

2. Design and installation

Agree access point locations, cabling and PoE needs, backbone connectivity, wireless configuration and management approach before deployment.

3. Configuration and validation

Configure SSIDs, authentication, VLANs and policies as scoped, then check coverage and connectivity in the agreed areas and document the handover.

What to Include in a Wi-Fi Installation Enquiry

These details help us understand your site and prepare a practical proposal:

  • Site location, building type and number of buildings or floors.
  • Floor plans or a marked-up layout showing the areas requiring coverage.
  • Approximate number of users and concurrent devices, if known.
  • Indoor, outdoor or point-to-point coverage requirements.
  • Existing internet connection, routers, switches, cabling and fibre backbone.
  • Required SSIDs, staff/guest separation, authentication and bandwidth policies.
  • Preferred equipment platform, project timeline and any BOQ or tender specification.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in an enterprise Wi-Fi installation?

Depending on the project, this may include requirements gathering, wireless site assessment, coverage planning, access point installation, controller or cloud management configuration, SSID and security setup, testing and handover.

Can you provide Wi-Fi for schools and multi-building campuses?

Campus wireless projects can be planned for multiple blocks and user groups. The design depends on building layout, outdoor coverage, backhaul availability, user density, applications and the required roaming experience.

Can you set up guest Wi-Fi with bandwidth management?

Guest wireless access, captive portal or hotspot authentication, user policies and bandwidth controls can be included where supported by the selected platform and agreed requirements.

Do you install wireless point-to-point links?

Wireless point-to-point or point-to-multipoint links may be considered where line of sight, distance, spectrum, capacity, mounting and regulatory requirements are suitable.

Does enterprise Wi-Fi require structured cabling?

Most access points require a suitable wired Ethernet connection and power arrangement, such as PoE where supported. Structured cabling and fibre backbone may form part of the wider network design.
