ACCESS CREDENTIALS

Biometric, Card and PIN Access Readers

Select a credential method to match the facility's security policy, user population and operating environment. Options may include fingerprint or facial biometric readers, proximity cards or fobs, PIN keypads, or multi-factor combinations where supported.

Reader selection should consider user throughput, indoor or outdoor conditions, credential administration, platform compatibility and the organisation's data-protection obligations.

DOOR HARDWARE

Electronic Locks and Door Release Devices

Door hardware may include electromagnetic locks, electric strikes, electric bolts, exit buttons, break-glass release units, door contacts and suitable door closers, selected for the door type and required operating mode.

Fail-safe or fail-secure behaviour must be selected for the specific opening and coordinated with emergency egress and applicable life-safety requirements. Hardware suitability should be verified on site before installation.

CONTROL & MANAGEMENT

Door Controllers and Access Management

Controllers and management software coordinate readers, door inputs, lock outputs, users, credentials, schedules and access permissions.

Depending on the selected platform and licence, a deployment may support single-door or multi-door control, time schedules, access groups, event logs, operator roles and central management across a facility or multiple sites.

ATTENDANCE

Time and Attendance System Integration

Where supported by the selected devices and software, access readers may also support attendance-related workflows, such as clock-in and clock-out events or export of attendance records.

Attendance functionality, reporting rules, payroll links and data handling should be specified separately and confirmed for compatibility before equipment selection.

INTEGRATION

Access Control and CCTV Integration

Integrated security workflows may link access events with compatible video surveillance platforms, alarms or other building systems.

Integration depends on supported interfaces, licensing, network design and the capabilities of the selected products. We recommend verifying compatibility and agreeing the exact event, alarm and video functions before implementation. Explore our CCTV installation services.

LIFECYCLE SUPPORT

Access Control Maintenance and Upgrades

Existing installations may need reader replacement, door hardware adjustments, controller expansion, credential administration, power backup improvements or troubleshooting.

Maintenance scope can include reader and controller checks, lock operation, exit devices, door contacts, power supplies, event logs, user permissions and system configuration, subject to assessment.

Access Control for Different Facilities

Offices and commercial buildings

Manage staff and visitor access at main entrances, office suites, server rooms, stores and other restricted areas using appropriate credentials and access schedules.

Schools and institutions

Control entry to administration blocks, laboratories, stores, staff-only areas and other designated spaces, with access groups and operating schedules based on institutional policy.

Warehouses and industrial facilities

Manage access to loading areas, stock rooms, plant rooms, operational zones and perimeter entry points while considering door construction and site conditions.

Residential and managed properties

Access control for apartment entrances, shared facilities, management offices, parking access points and other common areas, subject to the building's door and egress arrangements.

Our Access Control Deployment Process

1. Site and door assessment

Identify controlled openings, user groups, door types, entry and exit requirements, power availability, network access and any life-safety interfaces.

2. System design and installation

Agree reader technology, controller capacity, locking hardware, power backup, cabling, management platform, credentials and integration scope before deployment.

3. Configuration, testing and handover

Configure access levels and schedules, test reader and lock operation, verify exit and emergency release functions, confirm agreed integrations and hand over operating guidance.

What to Include in an Access Control Enquiry

These details help us assess the requirement and prepare a practical proposal:

  • Project location, facility type and number of buildings or floors.
  • Number and location of doors or access points to be controlled.
  • Door construction and existing lock, handle or access hardware, if known.
  • Approximate number of users and preferred credentials: biometric, card/fob, PIN or a combination.
  • Required access schedules, user groups, visitor process and management locations.
  • Whether attendance reporting, CCTV, alarm or other system integration is required.
  • Existing network, cabling, power supply and backup arrangements.
  • Any emergency release, fire alarm interface or specific life-safety requirements.
  • Project timeline, BOQ/tender specification and ongoing maintenance needs.

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of access control systems can Mlina install?

Depending on the site and requirements, a system may use biometric fingerprint or facial readers, proximity cards or fobs, PIN keypads, or a combination of credentials. Devices must be compatible with the controller and management platform.

Can access control be installed on existing doors?

Existing doors may be suitable after checking their construction, frame, hinges, lock arrangement, power availability and required egress. A site assessment helps identify compatible hardware.

Can the system manage multiple doors and user permissions?

A suitable controller and management platform can support multiple doors, users, schedules and access levels within its licensed and technical capacity. Door count, user volume and management functions should be included in the specification.

Can access control integrate with CCTV or attendance systems?

Integration may be possible where the selected platforms provide compatible interfaces and the integration is included in the agreed scope. Compatibility should be verified before procurement.

What happens during a power or network failure?

Behaviour depends on the controller, backup power, lock type, configuration and life-safety requirements. Emergency egress and door release must remain compliant with applicable requirements.

Do you install biometric access control?

Biometric readers can be included where suitable and supported by the selected platform. The system operator must address lawful collection, notice, access, retention and protection of biometric data under applicable privacy and data-protection requirements.

Do you provide access control maintenance and upgrades?

Support may include reader and controller checks, lock and door hardware inspection, power supply checks, credential and permission review, event-log checks, fault diagnosis and system expansion, subject to assessment.
